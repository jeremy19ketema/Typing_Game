About Typing Game

Learn how to play and improve your typing skills

How to Play

1

Set Up

Enter your name and select your preferred difficulty level. Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard word lists.

2

Start Game

Click "Start Game" to begin the 60-second timer. The input field will become active and ready for typing.

3

Type Words

Type the highlighted word exactly as shown and press Space or Enter to submit. The game will automatically advance to the next word.

4

Track Progress

Watch your WPM (Words Per Minute), accuracy, and timer in real-time. Try to beat your personal best!

Difficulty Levels

Easy

  • Common English words
  • 3-5 letters per word
  • Perfect for beginners
  • Example: "the", "and", "you"

Medium

  • Technical and common words
  • 6-10 letters per word
  • Good for intermediate typists
  • Example: "browser", "function", "website"

Hard

  • Complex and long words
  • 10+ letters per word
  • Challenge for advanced typists
  • Example: "asynchronous", "phenomenon"

Scoring System

WPM (Words Per Minute)

Calculated as: (Correct Words ÷ Time in Minutes)

Only correctly typed words count toward your WPM score.

Accuracy

Calculated as: (Correct Words ÷ Total Attempts) × 100

Shows your typing precision percentage.

Pro Tips

  • Focus on accuracy first, then speed
  • Use proper finger positioning
  • Don't look at the keyboard
  • Practice regularly to improve muscle memory
  • Use the hint button if you get stuck on a word

Game Features

Game History

Your game results are saved locally in your browser. View your progress on the Stats page.

Dark/Light Mode

Toggle between themes for comfortable typing in any lighting condition.

Responsive Design

Play on desktop, tablet, or mobile phone. The game adapts to your screen size.

Pause & Resume

Need a break? Pause the game anytime and resume where you left off.
