About Typing Game

Learn how to play and improve your typing skills

How to Play

1

Set Up

Enter your name and select your preferred difficulty level. Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard word lists.

2

Start Game

Click "Start Game" to begin the 60-second timer. The input field will become active and ready for typing.

3

Type Words

Type the highlighted word exactly as shown and press Space or Enter to submit. The game will automatically advance to the next word.

4

Track Progress

Watch your WPM (Words Per Minute), accuracy, and timer in real-time. Try to beat your personal best!

Difficulty Levels

Easy

  • Common English words
  • 3-5 letters per word
  • Perfect for beginners
  • Example: "the", "and", "you"

Medium

  • Technical and common words
  • 6-10 letters per word
  • Good for intermediate typists
  • Example: "browser", "function", "website"

Hard

  • Complex and long words
  • 10+ letters per word
  • Challenge for advanced typists
  • Example: "asynchronous", "phenomenon"
